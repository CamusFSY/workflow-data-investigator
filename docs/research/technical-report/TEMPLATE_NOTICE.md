# NeurIPS style provenance

Official source page: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers

Its paper-template link points to:
https://media.neurips.cc/Conferences/NeurIPS2026/Formatting_Instructions_For_NeurIPS_2026.zip

The paper author supplied `neurips_2026.sty` through a verified source transfer
on 12 October 2026. Its SHA-256 is
`c3fc2894e83d2517ca18b66741d6c595986d97957dc08ec08bb2125a7ec4555a`.
The style is retained unmodified and used with the `preprint` option. Its
attribution comments credit Roman Garnett and earlier style authors.

This package includes the style source, not the original formatting ZIP or
its companion example manuscript and checklist. The cloud integration could
not retrieve that ZIP and does not claim to distribute absent files.

This notice records provenance and preserves upstream notices; it does not
invent or replace a license grant. Preprint formatting implies neither
submission nor acceptance. No conference checklist is represented as completed.
